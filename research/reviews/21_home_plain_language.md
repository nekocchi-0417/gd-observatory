# Home-page copy pilot

15 October 2026

The user requested a home-page-only trial before a wider copy review. The aim is accessible, professional writing: state what changed in complete sentences, define the quantity behind a number, and keep technical detail available without putting every qualification in the main text.

## Changes

- Replaced abstract chart headings with literal descriptions of task capability and employment.
- Explained the capability comparison in human-work time. The 209-fold ratio now appears only in the measurement note, with its two models, dates and fixed success rate.
- Kept the employment population visible: US workers aged 22-25 in highly AI-exposed occupations. Stated the 12.8% change with its start and end dates.
- Added a small notice above the pair explaining the limited coverage and absence of a causal conclusion.
- Added numbered references with short hover definitions and keyboard/touch-accessible notes below. Each note retains source and data links.
- Rephrased the comparison's interpretation as an open research question. Removed the compressed "x, not y" construction from the homepage.
- Left Emerging evidence and all research-gap pages unchanged pending feedback. All chart data are unchanged.

# Measurement synthesis and merged research gaps

15 October 2026

## Purpose

Pathways explained how the proposed steps connect; Research gaps collected evidence and unanswered questions. They are now one Research gaps destination: the diagram, a measured-versus-missing overview, and nine step pages. Old links remain supported.

Each step shows Currently measured and Measurement gaps in two responsive lanes. Seventeen compact indicator cards retain source links, scope, limits and the existing selection rationale. Twenty-three gap cards distinguish limited evidence, indirect measures and measures not tracked in this source selection. These are AI-assisted judgements, not the findings of a systematic review or proof that research does not exist elsewhere.

Large charts remain on Home and Emerging evidence. Topic pages retain source data, CSV exports, related evidence and the earlier study proposals in disclosures. No source has been newly verified by this layout change.

## Content decisions

- The unresolved Bank of England denominator remains in More evidence, not a main indicator card. Delegation therefore has one featured indicator.
- Adoption gaps distinguish observed work time from the self-reported assisted hours already covered by the linked RPS study.
- Skills cards explicitly compare the trial's adjusted results with the no-AI group; they do not imply a before-after decline or lasting population-wide deskilling.
- Culture distinguishes October's release with August figures, including peak-day uploads, from the January 2025-April 2026 daily-upload comparison. These are not interchangeable series.
- Employment retains all six age groups and the distinction between exposure and measured employer adoption.
- Human influence separates perceived control from observed decision authority and potential classifier-flagged disempowerment from confirmed harm.

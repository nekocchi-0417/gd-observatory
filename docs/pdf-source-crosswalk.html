What to defer

  • Historical chart arrays, revision-sensitive trends and derived statistics not rebuilt from a saved primary dataset.
  • Secondary reports, narrow preprints and company statements not needed for the two-source overview.
  • Medical deskilling case studies as population-wide AI evidence; keep setting and AI type explicit.
  • General literacy decline, market concentration, lobbying and trust measures as proof of AI-caused loss of influence.
  • Forecasts, proposed warning thresholds and proposed measurement instruments.

Promote later only after: checking the original finding and its denominator, confirming study design and limitations, and explaining what it adds beyond the current sources.
